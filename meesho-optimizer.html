IMAGE-ASSISTED PACKAGING OPTIMIZATION

Meesho Packaging & Weight Calculator

Upload a product photo, mark its boundaries, calibrate against a known measurement, then compare packaging and weight slabs. Calculations run locally. This tool estimates costs using your entered shipping rates; it cannot retrieve Meesho's live charges.

1. Product image & measurement

Drag a rectangle over the product in the image. A photograph alone does not establish real-world dimensions. Enter a known reference width for the selected rectangle or measure the product manually.

2. Shipping rate assumptions

Rates vary by marketplace, seller account, service area, logistics and time. Copy your actual Meesho charges into the fields below. The default rates are deliberately zero, not invented quotations.

3. Packaging options

Edit the packaging list below. Dimensions are usable internal dimensions in centimetres. Each line: name,length,width,height,weight in grams,cost in ₹. Replace sample sizes and costs with your actual stock.

Enter measured dimensions and weight, then compare. The photo is optional.

Important: Image width calibration works only for the selected plane at a similar distance from the camera. Perspective, flexible products and packaging compression can make image-derived dimensions inaccurate. Verify with a ruler and weighing scale. Do not misdeclare weight or dimensions to the courier.
